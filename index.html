The History of Hip Hop

A quick look at where hip hop came from and how it grew.

Where Hip Hop Started

Hip hop started in the Bronx in New York City during the 1970s. Young people used music, dancing, and art as ways to express themselves and bring people together.

DJs played a big part in the beginning of hip hop. They would play music at parties and focus on the best parts of songs. Over time, MCs, breakdancers, and graffiti artists became important parts of hip hop culture.

Parts of Hip Hop Culture

  • DJing
  • MCing and rap
  • Breakdancing
  • Graffiti art
