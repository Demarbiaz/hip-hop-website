Hip Hop Through the Years

How hip hop grew from a local culture into a worldwide movement.

The Growth of Hip Hop

During the 1980s and 1990s, hip hop became more popular across the United States. More artists started making rap music, and hip hop began appearing on radio stations, television, and in movies.

Hip hop continued to change as new artists created different styles of music. It also became popular in other countries. Today, hip hop is one of the most well-known types of music and culture in the world.

Important Steps in Hip Hop's Growth

  1. Hip hop began in the Bronx during the 1970s.
  2. Rap music became more popular during the 1980s.
  3. Hip hop became a major part of popular culture during the 1990s.
  4. Hip hop spread around the world.
  5. Hip hop continues to influence music and culture today.
